Our Academics

A Curriculum That Grows With Your Child

From first words and numbers in Nursery to a full academic programme by Primary 6, every stage builds on the last.

Nursery Section

Age 2 – 5

Play-based learning built around phonics, numbers, colours and shapes, alongside social skills and character development. Children learn through guided play, songs, stories and hands-on activity.

Primary Section

Primary 1 – 6

A comprehensive curriculum following Nigerian national standards, with strong emphasis on Mathematics, English Language, Basic Science and Moral/Christian Religious Education.
