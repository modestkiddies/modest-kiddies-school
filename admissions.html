Admissions Made Simple

Five steps from your first enquiry to your child's first day in class.

1

Inquire

Reach out by phone, email or the contact form and tell us about your child — their age and the class they'd be joining.

2

Tour the School

Visit our campus, meet the teachers, and see a real classroom in session before you decide.

3

Assessment & Interview

A short, friendly placement conversation with your child (and parents) to find the right class fit.

4

Offer & Registration

Receive your offer, complete registration paperwork, and settle applicable fees.

5

Welcome Orientation

Meet your child's class and teacher ahead of their first official day.

What You'll Need

Registration Requirements

  • Birth certificate or age verification
  • 2 recent passport photographs
  • Immunization record
  • Previous school report (for transfers)
  • Completed registration form
  • Registration fee at enrollment
